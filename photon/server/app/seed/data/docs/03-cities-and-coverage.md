# Cities and Coverage

Meridian currently operates in:

- Bangalore
- Mumbai
- Delhi
- Singapore
- Dubai

Service hours are 06:00–23:00 local time in every city. Bookings outside
those hours are rejected at creation time with a 422.

New city launches go through a checklist owned by Ops: local timezone
registration, base fare calibration, and (if applicable) partner agreement
sign-off. Ping #ops-expansion if you're a customer asking about a city
that isn't listed above.
