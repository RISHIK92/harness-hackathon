# Partner Program

Meridian's partner program covers two kinds of relationships:

1. **Partner-tier customers** — businesses that book through Meridian and
   receive preferential commission rates as part of a negotiated agreement,
   usually tied to a regional operator relationship or guaranteed volume.
2. **Reseller/referral partners** — local operators who refer bookings to
   Meridian or provide fleet capacity in a given city, in exchange for
   revenue share or preferential rates for their referred accounts.

Partner agreements are negotiated by the Partnerships team and require
sign-off from Finance before a rate change ships. Active agreements are
tracked internally; ask in #partnerships if you need the current list or
the terms of a specific deal.
