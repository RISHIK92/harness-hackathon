# Account Tiers

Meridian accounts are assigned one of three tiers by Sales at onboarding:

| Tier | Who it's for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `standard` | Most customers | Base commission rates apply everywhere |
| `partner` | Customers with a regional reseller or referral agreement | Preferential commission in specific cities where Meridian has a partner agreement in place |
| `enterprise` | High-volume customers on a negotiated contract | Flat 5-point commission reduction on top of any city-level rate |

Tier changes go through Account Management and take effect at the start of
the next billing cycle. Partner-tier eligibility in a given city depends on
whether Meridian has an active reseller agreement there — see your Partner
Success contact if you believe your city should qualify.
